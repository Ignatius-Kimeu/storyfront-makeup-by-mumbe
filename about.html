The artist

Mercy Mumbe

Freelance, Nairobi-based, and booked almost entirely through a phone number in an Instagram bio.

The short version

A thousand posts is a lot of practice.

Mercy Mumbe works as a freelance makeup artist across Nairobi — brides and their families, graduates, portrait sessions, and creative work for brands and television. She travels to where the morning is happening, which is usually a house or a hotel room somewhere with bad lighting and a lot of people.

Her Instagram is 1,079 posts deep and her TikTok has been liked 443,400 times. Two of her running series tell you where the volume is: a graduation glam playlist twenty-eight films long, and a bridal reaction playlist of thirty-six.

In 2024 she was nominated for Make-Up Artist of the Year at the People's Choice Awards Kenya. Her note to her followers about it is the least self-promotional thing on her account: "it's not my win alone, it's our win."

Most of the job looks like this, and almost none of it makes the reel.

How she works

Three things worth knowing before you book.

01

She comes to you

Bridal mornings happen where the bride is. Hotel rooms, family homes, venues — she packs for the room she's given.

02

Trials are worth it

For brides especially. An hour in advance removes every argument on the actual morning, and it's the only way to test how a look photographs on you.

03

Quoted per session

Headcount, location and start time change the number. Send those three things and you'll get a straight answer.
